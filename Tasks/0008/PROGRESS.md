## Done (second look pass)

- **Sampled reference.** Colours were sampled from the on-screen still U13 and
  recorded in REFERENCES.md: neutral, slightly warm grey; lit decks about 145–165
  sRGB, sides about 105–110; strong plate contrast; ochre markings on only
  0.25 % of the silo area.
- **Procedural plating textures.** The base colour, ORM and normal maps (2048²,
  32 m tile, seed 304) are generated by Tools/Prepare-DaedalusDetail.py and
  applied through a world-scale box-projected `UVMap`. COLOR_0 now holds a
  neutral palette, plate patches and AO.
- **Smaller fixes.**
  - The silo markings are a faint ochre tint on the outer door frame ridges
    only.
  - The hangar interiors are dark plated.
  - The windows and bow strip are cool blue-white.
- **Add-ons and turrets.**
  - `DaedalusAddOns.glb` holds the bridge masts and two forward bow rods (so
    the hull stays 600 m).
  - `DaedalusTurrets.glb` holds 38 twin-barrel railgun turret objects on the
    mount frames.
  - Two `ROD` mounts mark the rod tips.
- **Look-dev.** `LookDev_Series` in the .blend has a key sun, an Earth bounce, a
  camera fill and `CAM_SeriesStill` matching U13. It was compared iteratively
  against U13 with renders in `.local/daedalus-detail/look` (ignored).
